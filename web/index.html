    Road surface & local direction

    Road model ready to load.

    A compact SegFormer model predicts road surface once per fresh photo. Road centerlines and supported vehicle corridors provide a local image axis; vehicle facing chooses a supported direction along it. Intersections and conflicting evidence abstain. This estimates road alignment, not one-way rules, speed or measured movement.

    ISOLATED VEHICLE / SINGLE PHOTO

    Estimated vehicle facing

    Crop orientation model ready to load.

    A compact car model trained on labeled isolated crops checks the original and mirrored view together. Difficult crops get a full-resolution second opinion. Paired white headlights or red taillights provide additional evidence. Facing estimates do not prove movement, stopping, speed or compass direction.

    Model & evidence sources

    Fast MobileNetV3 facing model (~16 MB), trained with low-resolution and lighting augmentations. WebGPU is tried first, including GPUs without FP16; CPU is used only if the browser cannot supply a working GPU path, with the reason shown in status. Uncertain crops load the larger CFV model on demand (~96 MiB FP16 GPU, ~192 MiB FP32 GPU, or ~55 MiB CPU). Both paths check mirror and angle/view agreement. Validation uses separate car identities; DriveBC accuracy remains unmeasured.
